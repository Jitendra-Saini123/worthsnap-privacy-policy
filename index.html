Privacy Policy for WorthSnap

📅 Effective Date: October 23, 2026 📱 Package ID: com.worthsnap.worth_snap 👤 Developer: Jitendra Saini
Summary: WorthSnap is an AI-powered resale appraisal and treasure appraisal tool designed to identify items, inspect conditions, and estimate fair market resale values. We respect your privacy. We do not sell your personal data, we do not perform facial recognition, and your photos are processed ephemerally solely to return appraisal estimates.

1 Information We Collect & How We Use It

To provide accurate appraisal and valuation services, WorthSnap collects the following information:

  • Images & Camera Captures: When you photograph an item or choose an image from your photo gallery, the image is transmitted securely via HTTPS to our valuation engine for visual object recognition, condition inspection, and price research.
  • Valuation History: The results of your appraisals (item title, brand, estimated prices, condition summary, listing drafts) are stored locally on your device in your personal History Vault. You can delete these records at any time.
  • Device & Diagnostics Data: Non-personally identifiable diagnostic data (such as app crash logs, device model, and OS version) to ensure high performance and fix bugs.

2 Device Permissions

WorthSnap requests explicit user permissions on Android to operate core features:

  • Camera Permission (android.permission.CAMERA): Required exclusively when you choose to take a photo of an item using the in-app camera viewfinder.
  • Photo / Storage Access: Required only when you select an existing image from your device gallery to appraise.
  • Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET): Required to send image data to the appraisal engine and retrieve real-time resale price estimates.

You can revoke these permissions at any time through your device's Settings > Apps > WorthSnap > Permissions.

3 AI Processing & Third-Party Service Providers

WorthSnap utilizes secure industry-standard cloud providers to perform AI valuations:

  • Google Gemini / Genkit Services: We process visual item attributes using Google Gemini multimodal AI models to identify brand, model, and secondary marketplace value. Images sent to the AI model are analyzed strictly to return appraisal results and are not retained to train public models.
  • Google Play Services: Used to manage app distribution, app integrity verification, and optional in-app subscriptions / purchases in accordance with Google Play developer policies.

4 Data Retention & Deletion

We believe in minimal data footprint:

  • Appraisal photos and results are cached locally on your device for your viewing convenience.
  • You can clear your appraisal history at any time by tapping Clear History inside the History Vault screen or clearing the app storage via Android settings.
  • If you have any questions or wish to submit a data inquiry, contact us at the email listed below.

5 Children's Privacy

WorthSnap is intended for a general audience interested in secondhand shopping, thrifting, and resale valuation. We do not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided personal data, we will take immediate steps to delete such information.

6 Security Measures

All data exchanged between the WorthSnap mobile application and cloud backend servers is encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2 / TLS 1.3 - HTTPS) protocols. We implement rigorous administrative and technological safeguards to protect against unauthorized access.

7 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our services or legal obligations. Any updates will be posted directly to this page with a revised "Effective Date" at the top.
